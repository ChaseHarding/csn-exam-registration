# Requirements

31 requirements gathered from stakeholder prompts. Each one has a priority and a test script used to verify it.

| ID | Category | Requirement | Source Prompt | Priority | Test Script |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ01 | Business Rule | The system shall prevent two sessions from being scheduled in the same room at overlapping times | How does the system prevent two sessions from being scheduled in the same room at overlapping times? What does faculty see when they try? | High | Create a session in a room from 10AM-11AM. Try to create in the same room for 10:30AM-11:30AM and confirm it is rejected. |
| REQ02 | Business Rule | The system shall not show a session to a student that has reached its max seat capacity. | What does a student see when a session is full? Is it hidden, or shown as full? | High | Fill a session to its capacity, log in as another student and confirm that the session is no longer visible. |
| REQ03 | Email | The system shall let faculty compose the confirmation email template, personalized to each student. | What can faculty change in the confirmation email template, and which parts are filled in automatically for each student? | Medium | Edit the email template, register a test student, and confirm the emails shows that students information. |
| REQ04 | Reports | The system shall let faculty export reports as CSV, Excel, or PDF depending on the report type. | Which export format (CSV, Excel, or PDF) goes with which report? | Medium | Export one report in each format, open them, and confirm the data is correct. |
| REQ05 | Users/Roles | The system shall provide separate access for students and faculty. Faculty functions are only available on through a separate portal | Who are all the types of people who will use this system? | High | Log in as a student and verify no faculty functions are available. Log in as a faculty member and confirm session management is available. |
| REQ06 | Authentication | The system shall authenticate a student by Email as the username and NSHE ID as the password. It will validate through the data provided by the school. | What happens when a student enters a wrong email or NSHE ID? | High | login with matching Email and NSHE ID and confirm you have access granted. |
| REQ07 | Authentication | The system shall deny access when the email or NSHE ID do not match any student data. | What happens when a student enters a wrong email or NSHE ID? | High | Enter a wrong email, then a wrong NSHE ID and confirm you are not granted access. |
| REQ08 | Registration | After login, the system will display the students first name, last name, and ID at the top of the form. | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | login as a student and confirm your first name, last name, and ID appear at the top of the screen. |
| REQ09 | Registration | The system shall allow students to select a campus from a dropdown menu. | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Open the form and confirm a campus dropdown list for the three CSN campuses appears. |
| REQ10 | Registration | The system shall only show dates that have sessions in the date dropdown | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Select a campus and confirm every date listed has at least one available session. |
| REQ11 | Registration | The system shall show only the times available at the selected campus and date in the time dropdown | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Select a campus and date, then confirm the times match the sessions created for that campus and date. |
| REQ12 | Registration | The system shall only show the approved exams for the selected session in the dropdown | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Select a time and confirm only the approved exams for that session appear. |
| REQ13 | Data Quality | The system shall not require any typed input from the student after logging in. Only dropdown menus. | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Complete one registration and confirm there is no field required with a text input after login. |
| REQ14 | Registration | The system shall let the student submit a session registration once all selections are made. | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Complete all dropdown selections, submit, and confirm the registration is saved. |
| REQ15 | Email | The system shall send an email confirmation to the student's CSN email after they submit. The email includes student name, exam name, campus, building, room, date, start time, and end time. | What are the students steps from login to confirmation | High | Fully register, then open your email and confirm all eight items listed are present in the email. |
| REQ16 | Sessions | The system shall let faculty select a proctor for an exam from a proctors list | Who are all the types of people who will use this system? | High | Open a session creation and confirm there is a proctor dropdown list from the loaded file of proctors. |
| REQ17 | Exams | The system shall let faculty add/remove exams on the approved exam list each semester | Who are all the types of people who will use this system? | High | Add an exam and confirm it appears for students. Remove that exam and confirm it no longer appears for students. |
| REQ18 | Reports | The system shall let faculty run a report by instructor, student, exam, location, or date. | Who are all the types of people who will use this system? | High | Run each report type and confirm the report matches the known data. |
| REQ19 | Campuses | The system shall support the West Charleston, North Las Vegas, and Henderson campuses, each with multiple buildings and rooms. | What buildings and rooms exist at each campus, and how are they set up in the system? | High | Create a session for each campus and confirm each one can be selected. |
| REQ20 | Scope | The system shall not include exam delivery, scoring, attendance, or payment processing. | Preliminary Report | Low | Review each screen and confirm none of these functions exist |
| REQ21 | History | The system shall let students review their own registration history for the current semester. | What does a student see in their registration history? Are cancelled registrations included? | Medium | Register two students for the same session, Log in as a student with a registration and confirm only that one registration for their account appears in the history. |
| REQ22 | Cancellation | The system shall make a cancelled seat available again for other students. | When a student cancels, does the seat open up right away for other students? Is an email sent? | Medium | Fill a session, cancel a registration, and confirm the session appears again for another student. |
| REQ23 | Business Rule | The system shall allow a student to register for only one session per exam name. Once a student is registered, that exam will not appear in the dropdown for that student regardless of time, campus, or date. | What happens if a student tries to register for an exam they're already registered for? | High | Register a student for an exam. Start a new registration as the same student and register for the same exam at a different campus or time/date. Confirm the same exam does not appear in the dropdown. Log in as a different student and confirm the exam is listed. |
| REQ24 | Cancellation | The system shall let a student cancel their own registration online by logging in, finding their registration and cancelling it. There is no deadline, and a student can cancel as late as 5 min before the exam. | How does a student cancel a registration, and what is the exact cutoff time before the session? | High | Register a student for an exam in 30min-1hour. Log in as the student and find the exam and confirm it can be cancelled. Repeat the process with an exam starting in 5 minutes and confirm you can cancel it. |
| REQ25 | Data | The system shall delete all data at the end of each semester. This includes student data, registration data, instructor/proctor data, and session data. | At the end of the semester, exactly what data is deleted and what is kept? Does registration history go too? | High | Load sample data for students and proctors. Create a session and register a student. Then, run the end of semester wipe and confirm all previous data is deleted. |
| REQ26 | Usability | The system shall provide a student a single-page form screen with drop down menus so a student can complete an exam registration without navigating multiple pages. | What web browsers or devices does the system need to work on? | High | Log in as a student and complete a registration. Confirm every step happens on one screen and the only free input is login, everything else should be dropdown menus. |
| REQ27 | Data Loading | The system shall load a student file (last name, first name, NSHE ID, CSN Email) and an instructor/proctor file (NSHE ID, name, and role) at the start of each semester. | What fields are in the student file and the instructor/proctor file? | High | load sample files. Confirm a listed student can log in and a listed proctor/instructor appears in the dropdown. |
| REQ28 | Sessions | The system shall let faculty create a session by entering the date, start time, end time, campus, building, room, proctor, and seat capacity, and it will validate before saving | List every field faculty enters when creating a session, and which ones are required | High | Create a session with all fields filled out and confirm it saves and appears in the dropdown for students. |
| REQ29 | Reports | The system shall give each proctor a report of the students registered for their exam sessions and the exams they are taking. | Who are all the types of people who will use this system? | High | Register two students for one proctors session, run the report. Confirm both students appear in the report. |
| REQ30 | Sessions | The system shall allow faculty to adjust the capacity of seats available when creating a session. | Can faculty change the 20-seat default when creating a session? | Medium | Log in as a faculty member, create a session and set the capacity to 25. Confirm the session has 25 seats available. |
| REQ31 | Sessions | The system shall set a session's default capacity as 20 seats. | Can faculty change the 20-seat default when creating a session? | Medium | Create a session without changing the capacity and confirm the seating is at 20 by default. |
